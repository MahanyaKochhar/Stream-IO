# General Consult

Use with the shared checklist in `SKILL.md`.

The consultation question, symptoms, examination, imaging, and treatment response
are covered by the shared checklist. Capture the clinician’s stated diagnosis,
including suspected or unconfirmed disease. Do not require MRI or failed
conservative treatment for every consultation.

## Requirement definitions

```yaml
requirements:
- id: consult.working_diagnosis
  description: Working diagnosis
  guidance: Referring clinician’s working diagnosis and diagnostic uncertainty.
```
