# Surgical Evaluation

Use with the shared checklist in `SKILL.md`.

The surgical question, functional limitations, conservative treatment response,
prior surgery or hardware, and medical background are shared requirements. These
additional fields support specialist review; do not determine surgical candidacy
or infer a patient’s goal from the referring clinician’s request.

## Requirement definitions

```yaml
requirements:
- id: surgery.patient_goal
  description: Surgical goals
  guidance: Patient’s stated goal or expectation for surgery.
- id: surgery.risk_factors
  description: BMI and tobacco use
  guidance: Documented BMI and tobacco use relevant to operative assessment.
```
