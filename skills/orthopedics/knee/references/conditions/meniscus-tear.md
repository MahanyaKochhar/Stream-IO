# Meniscus Tear

Use with the shared checklist in `SKILL.md`.

Use these additional findings for documented or suspected meniscal injury.
Mechanical symptoms, imaging and tear details, and prior surgery are shared
requirements. Record tear details only as reported; do not infer repairability or surgical
candidacy. If MRI was not performed, document that limitation for tear morphology.

## Requirement definitions

```yaml
requirements:
- id: meniscus.exam_findings
  description: Meniscal examination
  guidance: Joint-line tenderness and McMurray or Thessaly findings.
```
